Decision Intelligence,
Simulation, and
Healthcare Operations.

At DISH Lab, we study how to make better decisions in the era of big data and AI—with AI and for AI. Through simulation and digital twins, we model and optimize complex systems under ambiguity and uncertainty. In healthcare, we aim to address unmet needs in the design and delivery of care, connecting methodological advances with operational challenges.
